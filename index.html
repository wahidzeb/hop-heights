About Hop Heights

Hop Heights is a bright 3D obstacle course in the sky. Run, jump and balance your way across six worlds of floating platforms, jump pads, slippery ice and spinning bars.

Every stage has stars to collect, and enough stars unlock new skins for your hopper, all the way up to the Galaxy skin.

3 quick tips

  1. Look at the next platform, not your hopper, and aim toward where you want to land.
  2. Run before you jump. A standing jump covers much less distance than a jump from a short run-up.
  3. For long gaps, jump from the very edge of a block and hold the button all the way.

How to play

🕹️ Left side: drag to run. Jump button on the right: tap to hop, hold to jump higher. Drag the right side to turn the camera.

⌨️ Keyboard: WASD / arrows to run, Space to jump (tap to hop, hold to jump higher), drag the mouse or Q / E to turn the camera.

🏁 Reach the glowing checkpoint pad at the end of every stage. Fall off and you pop back to your last pad.

🔥 Avoid red glowing blocks and lava. Bounce on jump pads, ride moving platforms, climb ladders and watch out for ice!

⭐ Grab stars for new skins and trails in the Shop.
