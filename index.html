How to play

🕹️ Left side: drag to run. Jump button on the right. Drag the right side to turn the camera.

⌨️ Keyboard: WASD / arrows to run, Space to jump, drag the mouse or Q / E to turn the camera.

🏁 Reach the glowing checkpoint pad at the end of every stage. Fall off and you pop back to your last pad.

🔥 Avoid red glowing blocks and lava. Bounce on jump pads, ride moving platforms, climb ladders and watch out for ice!

⭐ Grab stars for new skins and trails in the Shop.
